Install & use offline

On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On your computer, use your browser’s Install app option if available. Open the app online once and wait for “Offline ready” before using it without internet. Keep backups: browser data can be cleared by your device or browser.

How percentages are calculated

Tasks: completed ÷ all tasks for the week. Bookings: non-cancelled bookings ÷ weekly target. Show-up rate: attended or sale ÷ attended, sale and no-show. Close rate: sales ÷ attended and sale. Goals: actual ÷ target, capped at 100% for completion. Income: generated ÷ target; can exceed 100%. No target or no calls displays a dash.
